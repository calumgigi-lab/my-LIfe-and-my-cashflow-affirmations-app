The App

Your monthly affirmations, always with you

My Life & My Cashflow Affirmations is the official app from Zion House INT'L. It carries the same monthly declaration booklets you receive in church — one day at a time, on your phone, with reminders so you never miss a morning confession.

Read today's declaration

Each morning, open the app to the affirmation for that calendar day in your current month's booklet.

Build your library

Unlock a new month when you're ready. Purchased booklets stay in your library for reading anytime, including offline.

Pay securely inside the app

Checkout with OasisPay. Once your payment is confirmed, the full booklet opens immediately — no queues, no paperwork.

Stay part of the family

Church announcements, testimonies, streak tracking, and the affirmers leaderboard keep you connected beyond Sunday.
