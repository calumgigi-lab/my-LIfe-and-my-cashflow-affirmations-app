FAQs

Common questions

Is the app free to download?

Yes — the app is completely free to download on Google Play. You only pay when you unlock a monthly booklet (₦1,500 per booklet).

Can I access old months after purchasing?

Absolutely. Once you unlock a booklet, it lives in your library forever and can be read offline at any time.

What payment methods are accepted?

In the app, payments are processed via OasisPay (Nigerian bank transfers and cards). On the web, we use Paddle, which accepts major international cards, PayPal, and more.

Is this a recurring subscription?

No automatic subscription — you choose and purchase each month's booklet individually when you're ready.

What is your refund policy?

We offer refunds within 7 days of purchase if you have not yet accessed the booklet, or in the case of a technical failure. See our full Refund Policy.

Who writes the affirmations?

Every declaration is crafted by Pst. Chinedum Ilechukwu, Man of God and voice to nations, based at Zion House INT'L, Port Harcourt, Nigeria.
